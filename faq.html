Frequently Asked Questions

Our pricing is based on a standard 13-gallon kitchen bag, which holds approximately 15 lbs of laundry. How much you fill it is completely up to you!

Our pricing is simple — $20 for your first bag and $15 for each additional bag, plus a flat $5 pickup and delivery fee per order. Your minimum order total is $25. No hidden fees.

The only additional fees are for same-day delivery, or if you require items to be hung — in which case we charge for hangers to make your experience as seamless as possible.

We currently accept Cash, Cash App, Zelle, and PayPal. We are also looking to expand to card transactions in the future!
