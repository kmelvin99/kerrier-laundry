FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, pickup, detergents and turnaround. Can't find your answer? We're a call or text away.

All questions

  • Our pricing is based on one standard kitchen-size trash bag, which holds about 15 lbs of laundry. How much you fill it is completely up to you!

  • Our pricing is simple: $20 for your first bag and $15 for each additional bag, plus a flat $5 pickup and delivery fee per order. Your minimum order total is $25. No hidden fees.

  • The only additional fees are for same-day delivery, for addresses more than 15 driving miles from Franklin ($0.89 per mile beyond 15, rounded up to the next whole mile), or if you require items to be hung, in which case we charge for hangers to make your experience as seamless as possible.

  • We currently accept Cash, Cash App, Zelle, PayPal and tap to pay.

  • We do! We offer 10% off your first service, and 10% off for customers who opt in to recurring services. Sign up for recurring service on your first order and get 20% off that first order (for example, $25 becomes $20.00). Discounts apply to your full order total, including the pickup & delivery fee. We also run promotions regularly, so keep an eye on our socials for those deals!

  • It depends on the load. If a load only has one white item, we'll wash it together with the other clothes. However, if there's a large load of colored clothes and a separate load of whites, we'll wash them separately. We always use our best judgment to care for your clothes properly.

  • We do our due diligence to note every item that passes through our machines and handle each customer's clothes with care. If you notice something is missing, bring it to our attention right away. We'll search for the item first, and if unsuccessful, we'll do our best to compensate for the lost item.

  • Simply pack your laundry in your bag and we'll pick it up at your scheduled time. You can leave it on the porch or hand it directly to us when we knock, whatever works best for you!

  • We deliver your clothes within 24-48 hours of pickup. Need a specific drop-off time? Just let us know and we'll work around your schedule!

  • We provide the detergent, but you're welcome to request a specific detergent of your choosing. If it's something we don't already have, we'll go out and get it to make sure your clothes are done exactly to your liking, and it's all included in the listed prices!

  • Our standard detergents include trusted brands like Gain, Tide, and Arm & Hammer. If you have a preference or a sensitivity, just let us know!

  • You can book online through our booking page, call or text us at (270) 776-2464, or send us a DM on Facebook. Whatever is easiest for you!
